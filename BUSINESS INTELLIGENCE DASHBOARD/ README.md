\cf0 1. About My Project\
\pard\pardeftab720\sa240\partightenfactor0

\f1\b0 \cf0 My project is called 
\f0\b Business Intelligence Dashboard
\f1\b0 . I created it using Python to analyse sales data and understand how a business is performing.\
The project helps us find total sales, total profit, the best-selling products, the best-performing regions and monthly sales performance. It presents the results using tables and charts, making the data easier to understand.\
I used sample sales data from a fictional company for this project.\

\f0\b Pandas:
\f1\b0  I used Pandas to organise sales data into tables, calculate totals and analyse products, regions and categories.\

\f1\b0  I used SQL queries to retrieve and summarise information from the database.\

\f0\b CSV:
